8. Court credit assignment

Origin-linked pending records mature into replay entries only after outcome and next-state availability. Immediate cost, eventual bonus and attributed reward remain separate. No bonus is attached to an unrelated arrival action.

r_origin = -cost + gamma_day ** ((completion - origin)/86400) * bonus
TD_target = r_origin + gamma_day ** ((next_time - origin)/86400) * (1 - terminated) * Q_next

The discounted return identity is tested. This does not prove Markov sufficiency of the existing compact observation. A custom cohort-aware/recurrent learner remains future work; vanilla PPO/DQN must not consume the legacy arrival-reward stream.
