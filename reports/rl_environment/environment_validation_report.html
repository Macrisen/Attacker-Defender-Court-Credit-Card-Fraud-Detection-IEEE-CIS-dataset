Permitted numeric observables use fixed tanh(sign(x)·log(1+|x|)/10) plus a missing-value bit; no transformation statistics are fitted. 5. Rewards, budgets and delayed labels

Court receives −1 once per accepted investigation; at completion it receives +10 for verified fraud previously APPROVED, +3 for verified fraud previously FLAGGED, and no bonus for legitimate transactions. SKIP and blocked investigation actions cost zero. Audit costs and bonuses are logged separately and are not Court policy rewards. Defender business reward (+10/−20/−5/+1) is evaluation-only, distinct from Court reward.

Every 10,000 arrivals receive 200 tokens: 180 Court and 20 reserved audits. Court cannot spend the reserve. Audits use a seeded online Bernoulli probability 20/10,000 on APPROVED transactions not already investigated by Court, capped at 20 per window. Unused tokens expire at rollover. The cap is per full window, not a guarantee of a 2% rate for every short prefix. Pending investigations survive budget rollover.

Verification delays are continuous Uniform(86,400, 259,200) seconds, simulated rather than observed. Due results are released before the next transaction's observation; the step reward contains the associated completion events, each with its originating transaction/action ID. No past reward is retroactively edited. Time-aware discounting and delayed credit assignment still require design before PPO training.

The stream horizon truncates the Gym episode and retains pending records as censored. An explicit diagnostic drain then advances to each completion timestamp without processing additional transactions. Drain rewards are reported separately and are not injected into the truncated rollout. Reset archives pending internal checkpoints before starting an independent episode.

8. Limitations and next research decisions

  • Real attack transformations and valid ASR are blocked pending a defensible threat model and dependency constraints.
  • Verified Memory is selected by Court and capped approval audits. It is not a representative random sample; selection bias remains, and no inverse-propensity correction is claimed.
  • Reward values and delay distributions are research assumptions, not measured financial loss or investigation duration.
  • Pending checkpoints are retained within the process; durable restart/recovery and continual model updates are not implemented.
  • Gym compatibility and smoke tests do not establish policy effectiveness. Delayed-credit handling, variable-time discounting and independent temporal evaluation must be settled before formal PPO training.
  • The Python module boundary separates observations from internal state by API convention, not by a security sandbox against malicious agent code.
